# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The 30 days start on the delivery date shown in
tracking. Items outside the window are not eligible for a return, but may be covered by the warranty if defective.

## Condition of returned items

Items must be unused, unwashed, and in their original packaging with tags attached. Items that show signs of wear or use
may be refunded in part or declined.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Opened beauty and personal care products, for hygiene reasons, unless they arrived damaged or defective
- Pet food and treats once opened
- Gift cards
- Items marked Final Sale on the product page

## Damaged, defective or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or is not what you ordered, report it within 30 days of delivery. We will pay for
return shipping and send a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

## How to start a return

Ask the support assistant to start a return for the item in your order and give a short reason. A return request is
created for that order item and its status is set to `return_requested`. You will receive an email with a prepaid return
label for eligible returns.

## Return shipping cost

Return shipping is free for damaged, defective or wrong items. For other returns, a $6.99 fee is deducted from the
refund to cover the return label.

## Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the item, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
Original shipping charges are not refunded except for damaged, defective or wrong items. Refunds are only issued by our
returns team; the support assistant cannot promise a specific refund amount or approve exceptions.

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. To swap a size or colour, return the original item and place a new order.
