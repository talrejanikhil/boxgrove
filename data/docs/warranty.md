# Warranty Policy

## Coverage periods

| Category                                           | Warranty period                                         |
|----------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Electronics                                        | 1 year from delivery                                    |
| Home (appliances, lamps, vacuums)                  | 1 year from delivery                                    |
| Outdoors (tents, sleeping bags, backpacks, stoves) | 2 years from delivery                                   |
| Apparel and Kids                                   | 90 days from delivery                                   |
| Beauty and Pets                                    | Not covered, except for damaged or defective on arrival |

## What is covered

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use, such as a zipper that fails, a battery that
stops holding a charge, or a seam that splits.

## What is not covered

- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from misuse, accidents, or unauthorized repairs
- Water damage to products not rated as water-resistant
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function
- Batteries that degrade gradually over time beyond the first 6 months

## Making a claim

Contact support with your order number and a description of the problem, plus photos if the item is damaged. Approved
claims are resolved by repair, replacement, or refund at our discretion. We pay shipping on approved warranty claims.

## Relationship to returns

The warranty is separate from the 30-day return window. A defective item can still be handled under warranty after the
return window has closed.
