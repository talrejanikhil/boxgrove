# Shipping Policy

## Processing time

Orders are processed within 1 business day of being placed. Orders placed after 2:00 PM Eastern Time, or on weekends and
public holidays, begin processing the next business day. You will receive an email with tracking details once your order
ships.

## Shipping options and delivery times

| Option    | Delivery time        | Cost                                 |
|-----------|----------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Standard  | 3 to 5 business days | $5.99, free on orders of $50 or more |
| Express   | 1 to 2 business days | $14.99                               |
| Overnight | Next business day    | $24.99                               |

Delivery times start from the day the order ships, not the day it is placed.

## Where we ship

Boxgrove ships to all 50 US states and US territories. We do not currently ship internationally or to PO boxes for
Express and Overnight orders.

## Tracking

Every shipped order has a tracking number sent by email. An order's status moves from `processing` to `shipped` to
`delivered`. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show carrier updates after the order ships.

## Delays and lost packages

Weather and carrier delays can extend delivery times. If a tracked package has not arrived 7 business days after the
expected delivery date, contact support and we will open a carrier investigation and either reship the order or refund
it.

## Address changes

An address can only be changed while the order is still `processing`. Once an order has shipped, the address can no
longer be changed.

## Large items

Items over 40 lbs, including some outdoor furniture and robot vacuums, ship by freight carrier and may take 5 to 8
business days. Free standard shipping still applies to qualifying orders.
