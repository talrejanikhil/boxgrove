# Payment Methods

## Accepted payment methods

- Visa, Mastercard and American Express
- Discover
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- Boxgrove gift cards

We do not accept personal checks, cash on delivery, or cryptocurrency.

## When you are charged

Your payment method is authorized when you place your order and charged when the order ships. If an item is out of
stock, you are only charged for the items that ship.

## Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the shipping address and is included in the order total.

## Failed or declined payments

If a payment is declined, the order is not placed. Check the billing address and card details, or try a different
payment method. Pending authorisations from a declined payment drop off within 3 to 5 business days.

## Split payments and gift cards

Gift cards can be combined with one other payment method. Gift card balances do not expire and cannot be redeemed for
cash.

## Promo codes

One promo code can be used per order, and codes cannot be applied after an order is placed. The support assistant cannot
create or apply discounts.

## Security

We never store full card numbers. Payments are processed by a PCI-compliant payment provider. Support will never ask for
your full card number, CVV or password.
