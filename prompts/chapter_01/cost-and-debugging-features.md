# Cost and Debugging Features

us:

1. The monthly OpenAI bill could increase without warning.
2. A customer could report a wrong answer that we cannot reproduce.

investigate. For every feature, say what we would look at first, what
question it answers, and which docs page covers it. Order them by how
quickly they would get us to an answer. Put your answer as bullet points
