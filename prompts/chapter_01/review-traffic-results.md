# Review Traffic Results

Execute the script and then read the CSV. Act as a skeptical reviewer.
For each row, say whether the reply is correct, questionable, or wrong,
based on what Boxgrove is supposed to do and the data in the product
catalog and order database. Show the results as a table with the message,
the verdict, and a one-sentence reason. Then summarize the most common
kinds of problems.

If a reply cannot be judged from the CSV alone, mark it "cannot tell" and
say what we would need to know.
